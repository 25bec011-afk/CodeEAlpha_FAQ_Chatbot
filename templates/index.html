1. NLP preprocessing

Questions are lowercased and cleaned by removing URLs, punctuation, numbers and extra whitespace.

🔢

2. TF-IDF vectors

The cleaned FAQ questions are converted into numerical TF-IDF representations using scikit-learn.

🎯

3. Similarity matching

Cosine similarity finds the closest FAQ and a confidence threshold prevents weak matches.
